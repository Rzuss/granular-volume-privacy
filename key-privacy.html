Privacy Policy for Volume Control Full Range Key

Last updated: September 7, 2026

Volume Control Full Range Key ("the Key App") is a small companion app by an independent developer (Rotem Zussman, "the developer"). Its only purpose is to unlock the full quiet range in the Granular Volume app ("Volume Control: Quiet Dial" on Google Play) on the device where both are installed, and to show that status.

The short version

The Key App collects no data at all. It requests no permissions, has no internet access (it does not request the INTERNET permission), contains no analytics, no advertising, and no tracking of any kind, and stores nothing. Nothing ever leaves your device, because nothing is gathered in the first place.

What the Key App does

The Key App displays a status screen: whether the main app is installed on this device. That check uses Android's standard package manager and happens entirely on your device. Separately, the main app checks, also locally through Android's package manager, whether the Key App is installed and was signed by the developer; that is how the unlock works. No server, network request, or account is involved in any of this.

Your purchase

You buy the Key App on Google Play like any other paid app. The payment is collected and processed entirely by Google under Google's own privacy policy (policies.google.com/privacy) and payments terms. The developer never receives, sees, or stores your payment details. To restore the purchase on a new device, install the Key App again from Google Play with the same Google account. For refunds, use Google Play's standard refund process or contact the developer at the email below.

Your rights

The developer holds no personal data about you, so there is nothing to access, correct, delete, or export on the developer's side under the GDPR, the CCPA, or similar laws. For data connected to the Google Play purchase itself, those rights are exercised against Google, which holds that data, through Google's own account tools.

Children's privacy

The Key App collects no data from anyone, including children, and contains no ads. Purchases on Google Play are subject to Google Play's own family and purchase-approval controls, and parents or guardians are responsible for supervising purchases made from accounts or devices a child can access.
