Privacy Policy for Granular Volume

Last updated: September 7, 2026

The trial and the unlock mentioned below are not in effect yet. The version of Granular Volume currently on Google Play is free to use, with no trial and no payment. Those parts describe version 1.5.0, which has not been released. Anyone who installs before it keeps the complete app free permanently. Nothing about how the app handles your data depends on which of the two applies: it has no internet permission either way.

Granular Volume ("the app") is a utility developed by an independent developer (Rotem Zussman, "the developer", "we") that adds fine-grained, software-level volume control on top of your Android device's built-in volume controls. This policy explains, in plain language, what the app does and does not do with information, and what rights you have. It covers the Granular Volume app on Google Play and F-Droid, and the optional companion app Volume Control Full Range Key ("the Key App"), which has its own short policy at the address given in the Key App section below.

The short version

Granular Volume does not collect, store, transmit, or share any personal data. The app has no internet access in any version: it does not request the INTERNET permission, contains no analytics, no advertising, and no third-party tracking SDKs of any kind. Nothing ever leaves your device. This is true for the free features, and it remains true after the optional full-range unlock: the purchase happens on Google Play's own store listing for the Key App, not inside this app, so this app never gains network access and never touches a payment.

Information we collect

None. The app's own code does not collect, and has no technical means of collecting or transmitting, names, email addresses, location, contacts, device identifiers, usage statistics, crash logs, payment details, or any other personal or sensitive information. It has no servers and no network capability of its own. We do not use cookies, analytics, advertising, or any tracking technology.

Information stored on your device

The app saves a small amount of local settings on your device only, so it can remember your preferences between sessions:

  • The on-screen position of the floating control.
  • Your last selected volume level.
  • Whether the control was active (used to optionally restart it after a reboot).
  • Whether you have accepted the current Terms of Use.
  • Local flags related to the optional full-range unlock: when the free trial on this device began, whether this device qualifies for the free full range as a long-time user, and which one-time informational screens have already been shown.

This data is stored privately in the app's own storage on your device and is never transmitted to us. We operate no servers and receive none of it.

One small part of it takes part in Android's own backup, and we want to be exact about which part. Android can copy an app's data into your Google account so that it comes back on a new phone or after a reinstall. We have limited that copy to a single file, which holds only three things: when your free trial began, a timestamp the app keeps so that record cannot be stretched by turning the device clock back, and whether this device qualifies for the free full range as a long-time user. Your settings, the position of the control, your volume level and every other preference are excluded from the backup and stay on the device they were made on.

That one file is what lets your trial, and a long-time user's free access, follow you to a new phone instead of being lost. The backup is between your device and your own Google account, under Google's terms and your Android backup settings, which you can turn off at any time. Nothing in it reaches us. Uninstalling the app removes its data from your device; the entitlement file may remain in your own Google account backup until Android's own retention removes it.

Permissions and why they are needed

  • Display over other apps (SYSTEM_ALERT_WINDOW): to show the floating volume control on top of other apps.
  • Modify audio settings (MODIFY_AUDIO_SETTINGS): to apply the volume attenuation to audio output.
  • Foreground service (FOREGROUND_SERVICE, FOREGROUND_SERVICE_SPECIAL_USE): to keep the control running reliably while you use other apps, with a persistent notification.
  • Run at startup (RECEIVE_BOOT_COMPLETED): to restore the control after the device restarts, only if it was active when the device shut down.
  • Notifications (POST_NOTIFICATIONS): to display the ongoing service notification required by Android for foreground services.

The app does not request the INTERNET permission in any version. None of the permissions above is used to access, collect, or transmit personal information.

The optional full-range unlock (the Key App)

The Google Play version of Granular Volume offers deeper quiet steps as an optional, one-time purchase. The purchase is not made inside this app. It works like this:

  • The unlock is a separate, small companion app called Volume Control Full Range Key, sold on its own Google Play listing. Buying and installing it is the entire unlock.
  • To decide whether the full range is unlocked, Granular Volume performs a purely local check, using Android's standard package manager, of whether the Key App is installed on this device and was signed by the developer. This check happens entirely on your device. Nothing is looked up online, and nothing is transmitted, by either app.
  • The payment itself is collected and processed by Google Play when you buy the Key App from its store listing, exactly like buying any other paid app. Payment details (card number, billing address, and similar) are handled by Google under Google's own privacy policy at policies.google.com/privacy. Neither Granular Volume nor the developer ever receives, sees, or stores any payment information.
  • The purchase is one-time. There is no subscription, no recurring charge, and nothing to cancel. To restore it on a new device or after a reset, install the Key App again from Google Play with the same Google account; Google Play remembers the purchase.
  • For refunds, you can use Google Play's standard refund process, or contact the developer directly at the email below.

The Key App itself collects nothing, requests no permissions, and has no internet access. Its own short privacy policy is published at rzuss.github.io/granular-volume-privacy/key-privacy.html.

The F-Droid version of Granular Volume includes the full range at no charge, contains no purchase feature of any kind, and performs no key check. It remains 100% offline, permanently.

Third-party services and components

The app is distributed through Google Play and F-Droid. Distribution, installation, and the optional purchase of the Key App are handled entirely by those platforms under their own respective privacy policies and terms. The app embeds no analytics, advertising, billing, or tracking SDK of any kind, and it has no internet access with which to reach one.

For completeness, the app is built on standard open-source Android libraries, which collect nothing and send nothing. There is one further component, and only in the Google Play version: Google's in-app review library, which is what lets the app show Google's own "rate this app" dialog. It is used at most once, it is optional for you, and it works by asking the Google Play app already on your device to display that dialog. It gives this app no network access, sends the developer nothing, and is absent entirely from the F-Droid version. A list of every component and its licence is available inside the app.

The website

The website granularvolume.com counts page visits with GoatCounter, a privacy-respecting open-source service. It sets no cookies, stores no IP addresses and builds no visitor profiles. It records, in aggregate, the page visited, the site that referred you, your country and your browser type. Clicks on the Google Play buttons on the site are counted the same way, so the developer can see which pages are useful. This applies to the website only: the app itself has no internet permission and sends nothing, exactly as described above.

Hosting and what the servers see. The website is served by GitHub Pages, a hosting service operated by GitHub, Inc. (Microsoft). Like any web server, GitHub's servers receive your IP address and the page you requested in order to deliver the page, and may keep that in their own server logs under GitHub's privacy statement; the developer does not receive or see those logs. GoatCounter's servers, which receive the page visit in order to count it, are operated by Hetzner Online GmbH in Finland and Germany, inside the European Union. GoatCounter does not store your IP address, does not store your full browser identifier, sets no cookie and stores nothing in your browser.

Why there is no consent banner. The counting described above stores no personal data and cannot identify a person, so it is done on the basis of the developer's legitimate interest in knowing which pages are useful, the same way a shop counts how many people came in. If you would rather not be counted at all, a content blocker that blocks gc.zgo.at will do exactly that and the site will work normally without it.
