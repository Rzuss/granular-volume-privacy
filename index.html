Privacy Policy - Granular Volume

Last updated: 21 June 2026

Granular Volume is a utility that adds fine-grained software-level volume steps on Android. This policy explains what the app does and does not do with information.

The short version

Granular Volume does not collect, store, transmit, or share any personal data. The app has no internet access - it does not request the INTERNET permission, contains no analytics, no advertising, and no third-party tracking SDKs. Nothing ever leaves your device.

Information we collect

None. We do not collect names, email addresses, location, contacts, device identifiers, usage statistics, or any other personal or sensitive information.

Information stored on your device

The app saves local settings on your device only: the floating control position, your last volume level, and whether the control was active. This data is never transmitted anywhere and is removed when you uninstall the app.

Permissions

  • Display over other apps - to show the floating volume control on top of other apps.
  • Modify audio settings - to apply volume attenuation to audio output.
  • Foreground service - to keep the control running with a persistent notification.
  • Run at startup - optional, to restore the control after device restarts.
  • Notifications - required by Android for foreground services.

None of these permissions collect or transmit personal information.

Children's privacy

The app collects no data from anyone, including children. No ads, no in-app purchases.
